Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes what data TILT processes, why it is processed, and the controls available to you when you use the app and this website.

What we collect

Depending on your usage, TILT may process: device identifiers, parent/kid pairing information, configured places and notification settings, geofence event records (for example enter/leave events), and push subscription identifiers required for notification delivery.

How we use data

Data is processed to operate core features, including device linking, geofence monitoring, event history display, delivery of push alerts, abuse prevention, service reliability, and troubleshooting.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be shared with service providers strictly as needed to run TILT (such as infrastructure hosting, push notification delivery, and billing/subscription processing).

Retention

Data is retained for the period required to provide features, enforce plan limits, maintain security, and support operations. Retention windows may vary by data category and plan tier; older data may be deleted, aggregated, or otherwise minimized.

Analytics cookies

With your consent, we use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand visits and pages viewed on this website. GA4 is not loaded and no analytics cookies are stored until you select Accept. You can refuse or withdraw consent at any time through Cookie settings in the footer.

Your controls

You can control data use by editing/deleting places, removing linked devices, adjusting notification settings, revoking location permissions in device settings, or uninstalling the app. You may also contact us for privacy requests subject to applicable law.

Children

TILT is designed for family coordination under parent or legal guardian supervision. Parents are responsible for lawful use of location features and obtaining any permissions or consents required in their jurisdiction.

Contact

For privacy questions, rights requests, or data-related concerns, contact: contact@9h41.org
